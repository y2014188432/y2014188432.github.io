Who We Are

Professional Audio Visual Solution Manufacturer

TOP LED Group is a professional visual display and stage technology solution manufacturer serving global markets, built on strong manufacturing foundations in LED display systems, LED dance floor systems, and stage lighting equipment.

Within the group manufacturing system, we specialize in the production, sales, and service of high-quality LED Products.

Founded in 2013, we operate a 220,000-square-meter manufacturing facility supported by a team of 500+ professionals. Each year, we deliver more than 1,000,000 units of audiovisual and stage equipment to global clients.
