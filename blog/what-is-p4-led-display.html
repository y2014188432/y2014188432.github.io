What Is a P4 LED Display Screen?

Admin October 17, 2025 8 min read 3 Comments

If you're struggling to find a digital LED screen that delivers stunning, crystal-clear visuals for your business or event, understanding P4 LED displays is essential. This comprehensive guide will help you understand everything about P4 LED screens.

What Does P4 Mean in LED Displays?

The "P" in P4 stands for "Pixel Pitch," which refers to the distance between the center of two adjacent pixels, measured in millimeters. A P4 LED display has a pixel pitch of 4mm, meaning there is 4mm between each pixel.

Pixel pitch is one of the most important specifications to consider when choosing an LED display because it directly affects:

  • Image Resolution: Smaller pixel pitch = higher resolution = sharper images
  • Viewing Distance: Smaller pixel pitch = shorter optimal viewing distance
  • Price: Smaller pixel pitch = higher cost per square meter

Key Specifications of P4 LED Display

Here are the typical specifications of a P4 LED display:

  • Pixel Pitch: 4mm
  • Pixel Density: 62,500 pixels/m²
  • Module Size: 320mm × 160mm
  • Brightness: 1000-6000 nits (depending on indoor/outdoor)
  • Refresh Rate: 1920-3840 Hz
  • Viewing Angle: 140°/140° (H/V)
  • Lifespan: 100,000 hours

Applications of P4 LED Displays

P4 LED displays are versatile and can be used in various applications:

Indoor Applications

  • Retail stores and shopping malls
  • Corporate lobbies and conference rooms
  • Churches and houses of worship
  • Exhibition centers and trade shows
  • Hotel ballrooms and banquet halls

Outdoor Applications

  • Building facades and billboards
  • Transportation hubs
  • Sports stadiums
  • Public squares and plazas

"P4 LED displays offer the perfect balance between image quality and cost-effectiveness, making them one of the most popular choices for both indoor and outdoor applications."

How to Choose the Right P4 LED Display

When selecting a P4 LED display, consider the following factors:

  1. Installation Location: Indoor or outdoor? This determines the brightness and IP rating you need.
  2. Viewing Distance: The optimal viewing distance for P4 is 4-20 meters.
  3. Content Type: Video content requires higher refresh rates than static images.
  4. Budget: P4 offers good value, but consider your overall budget including installation and maintenance.

Conclusion

P4 LED displays are an excellent choice for many applications, offering a great balance of image quality, viewing distance, and cost. Whether you need a display for a retail store, corporate office, or outdoor billboard, P4 LED screens can deliver the performance you need.
